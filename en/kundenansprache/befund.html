Request an assessment for your project

How far apart are your specification, your process models and your code? The Kanonik assessment answers this for a concrete project – with evidence for every gap, reviewed together with your domain expert.

What you get

  • Confirmed gaps between specification, BPMN and code – for each gap the passage in the document, the element in the model and the line in the code
  • Tasks with acceptance criteria, ready for your ticket system
  • Traceability matrix from requirement via rule, process and message to class and test
  • on request an ArchiMate model and a domain model of the application
  • the read-only viewer with graph, search and code, as in the examples on this site

How it works

  1. Short call (30 minutes): goal, scope, confidentiality.
  2. Inputs: specification (Word, PDF or Markdown), process models (BPMN), access to the code – one fixed version.
  3. Comparison: Kanonik puts everything into a knowledge graph and compares the three views.
  4. Review: we go through the gaps together with your domain expert, who decides on each one.
  5. Result: assessment with confirmed gaps, tasks and matrix.

When it pays off

  • A legacy system is being replaced or modernised.
  • A supplier implements a specification and you want to secure the acceptance.
  • After several releases nobody knows for sure what the specification still covers.

Confidentiality

Your documents stay confidential and are deleted afterwards. On request the comparison runs entirely on your machines. A language model is only used if you approve it – the comparison itself runs without one.

Request

For the first pilot projects I am looking for two or three partners: business units, consultancies or software vendors. The price depends on the scope; after the call you receive a fixed-price offer.
