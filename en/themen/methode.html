Method

Stepwhat happens
PreprocessingThe specification is split into chapters, paragraphs and tables; every requirement has an identifier.
ExtractionA language model reads processes, rules and the domain model chapter by chapter; domain experts review and accept.
Reading code and BPMNClasses, methods, messages and process elements are read from the repository without a language model.
ComparisonRules compare the three views and report gaps with evidence.
CurationDomain experts confirm gaps or mark false alarms; the rules are sharpened from that.

The public demo shows the result for reading. Editing happens in the curator on your own machine.
